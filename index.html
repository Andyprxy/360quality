Ask us something else
Do you issue ISO certification?

No. We offer implementation and certification-readiness support, including process alignment and internal audits. Certification decisions and certificates are the responsibility of an independent certification body.

What is the difference between a gap analysis and an internal audit?

A gap analysis identifies the differences between your current system and the requirements you want to meet. An internal audit evaluates evidence of whether defined criteria are met and whether the management system is implemented and effective.

Can we engage you for a single service?

Yes. Your scope may be a focused gap analysis, a scheduled internal audit, a defined ISO implementation project or a statistical analysis assignment. Ongoing advisory can also be discussed based on your needs.

Which organisations can benefit from these services?

Any organisation seeking stronger processes, management-system readiness or data-informed improvement may benefit. We confirm the applicable standards, organisational context and scope before proposing work.

What happens after we request a consultation?

The first discussion should establish your objectives, timeframe, current position and deliverables needed. From there, the work can be scoped into a practical engagement and proposal.

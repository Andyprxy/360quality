OUR SERVICE

Clarity before investment.

A well-structured gap assessment reveals the distance between current practice and the requirements that matter. 360 Quality Solutions helps Pretoria organisations review existing evidence, pinpoint improvement priorities and plan a realistic route forward.

We review agreed standards or management-system criteria alongside the organisation’s actual processes, documents, interviews and available records. Findings are translated into a risk-informed action plan so teams can focus resources where they make the most difference.

The assessment scope and criteria are agreed before the review begins.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

When should we conduct a gap analysis?+

A gap analysis is useful before implementation, before an external audit, or when your organisation wants to understand why its controls are underperforming.

Will we receive a practical action plan?+

That is the intended output: a prioritised record of findings and recommended next steps, based on the agreed scope.

Is a gap analysis the same as certification?+

No. A gap analysis is an advisory assessment. Certification decisions are made by an independent certification body.
